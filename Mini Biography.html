Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

Early Life and Education

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, into a humble family. As a young boy, he worked hard and even sold newspapers to support his family. He was curious, hardworking and had a strong interest in science and mathematics. His simple beginnings taught him the value of hard work, discipline and determination.

Career and Achievements

Dr. Kalam became a renowned scientist and worked with DRDO and ISRO. He played an important role in India's space and missile programmes and was popularly known as the “Missile Man of India.” He became the 11th President of India and served from 2002 to 2007. He was also honoured with the Bharat Ratna in 1997 for his contributions to the nation.

Words that Inspire

Dr. Kalam's life inspires us to be aspiring, hardworking, determined, humble and confident. He encouraged young people to dream big and work sincerely to achieve their goals. His life teaches us that our circumstances should never stop us from achieving our dreams.
